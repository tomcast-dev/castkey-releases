Optional. A wide picture (like 1920×1080) works best. It's resized automatically and darkened so text stays easy to read.

Intro video optional

Plays full screen when customers open the app (any button skips it). Keep it short — about 5–15 seconds and under 15 MB. Host the MP4 anywhere with a direct link: your website, GitHub, or Dropbox (Dropbox share links are fixed automatically). It downloads once to each device, so it doesn't use their data every time.

Home screen look

Support inbox Off Customers message you from the app

Get a ping in Discord when a customer writes, so you know to check this inbox. In Discord: channel ⚙ Edit Channel → Integrations → Webhooks → New Webhook → Copy Webhook URL, then paste it here and Save. Use a private staff-only channel. The ping only says who wrote, not what they wrote. If you ever get junk posts in that channel, delete the webhook in Discord and paste a new one here.
